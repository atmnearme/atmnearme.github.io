# ATM Near Me

Free static website (English, US) that helps people find an ATM near them and understand withdrawal fees, limits and safety.

## Tools (all client-side, no data leaves the browser)

1. ATM Fee Calculator — withdrawal amount + fee gives total cost and fee percentage.
2. Cash Withdrawal Planner — monthly cash need and per-trip amount give trips needed and total monthly fees.
3. Travel Currency Converter — foreign amount, exchange rate and foreign fee give the total USD charge.
4. ATM Safety Checklist — 8 interactive safety items with a live safety score.
5. Bank vs ATM Comparison — own-bank vs out-of-network cost for a withdrawal, with daily-limit check.

Also includes a reference table of approximate ATM fees and daily withdrawal limits for 8 major US banks, 6 guides, and an FAQ.

All fee and limit figures are approximate estimates — check with your bank for current numbers.

Live at: https://atmnearme.github.io/
